Turn a green-screen video into a see-through video, so a person or object appears standing in your 360° tour with the room around them. It works on every phone, including iPhones.

1 Choose your video

Tips for filming
  • Use a smooth green (or blue) sheet or wall, with no creases or shadows. Light it evenly.
  • Keep the person a metre or two in front of it, so they don't cast shadows on it.
  • Avoid green clothes (or blue, with a blue screen), shiny things and very fine hair blowing about.
  • Keep the camera still, and keep it short: a few seconds to a minute works best on phones.

⬇ Download the video
  1. Upload it to the videos folder of your GitHub site (Add file → Upload files).
  2. In the 360° tour maker, add a spot, paste the file's path (for example videos/my-video.see-through.mp4) and make sure See-through video is ticked.
How does it work?

The video it makes is an ordinary MP4, twice as tall as your picture: your picture on top (on black), and a black-and-white “stencil” underneath that says which parts are solid. Every phone can play an MP4, and the tour tool uses the stencil to cut your picture out, so the room shows around it. If you open the file by itself it will look strange; that's normal.

Your video never leaves this computer. Works best in Chrome or Edge on a Windows or Mac computer.
