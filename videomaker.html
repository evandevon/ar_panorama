Tips for filming
  • Use a smooth green (or blue) sheet or wall, with no creases or shadows. Light it evenly. (Fix problems can rescue a rough one.)
  • Film a second or two with nobody in front of the screen before the person steps in. That “empty background” helps fix creases and patchy light.
  • Keep the person a metre or two in front of it, so they don't cast shadows on it.
  • Avoid green clothes (or blue, with a blue screen), shiny things and very fine hair blowing about.
  • Keep the camera still, and keep it short: a few seconds to a minute works best on phones.

      How does it work?

      The see-through MP4 is an ordinary MP4, twice as tall as your picture: your picture on top (on black), and a black-and-white “stencil” underneath that says which parts are solid. Every phone can play an MP4, and the tour tool uses the stencil to cut your picture out, so the room shows around it. If you open the file by itself it will look strange; that's normal.

      The WebM keeps real transparency inside the video, but only some browsers can show it, and iPhones can't. The GIF can only be fully solid or fully see-through, so soft edges become crisp ones; it has no sound and is limited to 256 colours a frame.

      Your video never leaves this computer. Works best in Chrome or Edge on a Windows or Mac computer.
