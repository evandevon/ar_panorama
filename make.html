360° QR Experience Maker

Add a 360° photo, put pictures, videos, sounds and words around the room, then print the QR code. Anyone who scans it looks around by moving their phone.

1 Choose your 360° photo

A Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox share link, a direct link to a JPEG, or a 360 video (MP4). Set sharing to Anyone with the link.

This photo has spots merged into it. To move or change them, open the version with them still separate.

Compare photospheres optional: a second photo to compare

Add a second 360° photo taken from the same spot: an old photo, or the same room on another day. Viewers drag a bar left and right to wipe between the two.

Drag the bar in the preview so you can see both photos, then turn this until things line up across it.

Type your own name for each side; it shows beside the bar. The second photo is on the left, your main photo on the right. Leave a box empty for no name on that side.

2 Add things to the room optional

Turn the preview until the crosshair (+) is where you want something, then choose what to add. You can drag it into place afterwards.

Tips
  • In the preview, drag a thing to move it, and pull its corners to resize it. Or use Smaller, Bigger and Move to crosshair on its card.
  • Something can wait behind a small dot that viewers reveal by looking at it: Appears → After looking at a dot.
  • Background sound: add a Sound, set it to play as soon as it opens, and tick Hidden and Loop.
  • Rarely needed settings (floating on the screen, pinning corners, masks) are under More options on each card.

      How lining up works

      Viewers get a Line up with this room option. Their camera shows the room with your photo faded over it; they turn until the two match, and the page tells them when it looks lined up. Then the spots sit on the real walls. It works best when the photo was taken from where viewers will stand (e.g. by the QR code) and the room still looks similar. Viewers can always skip it.
