      How lining up works

      Viewers get a Line up with this room option. Their camera shows the room with your photo faded over it; they turn until the two match, and the page tells them when it looks lined up. Then everything sits on the real walls. It works best when the photo was taken from where viewers will stand (e.g. by the QR code) and the room still looks similar. Viewers can always skip it.

      For listening tours: viewers scan the code, lock their phone and keep listening as they walk. Otherwise sounds pause when the screen goes off. (Phones always pause videos.)

      For teachers

      Class link library List the class’s links in a Google Sheet once, then pupils pick from them

      Make a Google Sheet with four columns, in this order: Number | Type | Name | Link (one link per row, under a heading row). Type is one of image, video, sound, 3D, link, document or room (a 360° photo; a capital first letter is fine). Leave Type empty to offer it everywhere. Share the sheet as Anyone with the link → Viewer and paste its address here. QR codes then only hold short numbers like #3, and fixing a link in the sheet fixes every tour that uses it.
