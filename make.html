How to place and edit spots
  • Turn the preview so the crosshair is where you want a spot, then press Add a spot.
  • In the preview, drag a spot to move it, and pull its corner tags to resize it. The Smaller / Bigger and Move to crosshair buttons do the same without dragging.
  • A spot can wait behind a small dot that viewers reveal by resting the crosshair on it (Appears).
  • For background sound, add an MP3 set to Sound only, plays as soon as it opens, with Hidden and Loop ticked.

    How lining up works

    Viewers get a Line up with this room option. Their camera shows the room with your photo faded over it; they turn until the two match, and the page tells them when it looks lined up. Then the spots sit on the real walls. It works best when the photo was taken from where viewers will stand (e.g. by the QR code) and the room still looks similar. Viewers can always skip it.
